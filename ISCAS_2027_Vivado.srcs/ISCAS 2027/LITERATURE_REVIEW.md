## Literature-derived caution

Event-driven simulation and neuromorphic chips are established areas. Therefore, “skip computations adaptively” by itself is too broad to support a novelty claim. A potentially differentiating result would need a precisely defined current/state error model, a tested controller with prespecified tolerances and falsification cases, actual operand isolation/enable behavior, and matched FPGA activity/resource/timing reports. This review has not demonstrated that such a contribution is new; a systematic search of photoreceptor-specific and sensitivity-guided hardware literature is still required before making a novelty claim.
